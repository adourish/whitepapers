Short alt: Diagram: the connection pool problem and its fix, side by side.

Long description: Two panels. Left, "The problem: each request takes a second connection": the till sends 6 orders at once to an order service with a pool of 5 connections. Requests 1 to 5 each hold one connection and wait for a second that never comes, then give up and lose their history snapshot. Request 6 cannot get any connection and the till tries again 4 times. Result: 6 requests waiting, 50 history snapshots lost, 1 of 6 people gave up, 21.6 seconds against 3.0 seconds for a first send. Right, "After the fix: one connection per request": requests 1 to 5 save the snapshot on the connection they already hold and give it back; request 6 waits a moment and then gets one; no retries. Result: 6 of 6 finished in 3.9 seconds, no snapshots lost. Same result on Java 17, Java 21 and Java 21 virtual threads.
